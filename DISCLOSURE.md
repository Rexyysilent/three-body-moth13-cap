# Computational and tool-use disclosure draft

This project used substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex, using GPT-5.6 Sol
(model identifier `gpt-5.6-sol`), for numerical exploration, source-code
generation and review, interval-certificate and release engineering,
literature/catalog screening, and report preparation.

scholarly or legal author. The mathematical existence and collisionlessness
claims do not rely on model authority: they rest on the included
outward-rounded interval computations and the analytic reversibility argument,
both of which are exposed for independent review and rerun.

Before manuscript submission, the accountable human author must review the
complete text, mathematics, code, and logs; accept responsibility for every
claim; and adapt this disclosure to the target venue's current policy. See
